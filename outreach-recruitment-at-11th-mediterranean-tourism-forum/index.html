Outreach Recruitment at the 11th Mediterranean Tourism Forum

Our team joined international leaders, investors and innovators at Hilton Malta to exchange ideas about the future of tourism across the Mediterranean.

The 11th Mediterranean Tourism Forum is under way at Hilton Malta, bringing together international leaders, investors and innovators to exchange ideas about the future of tourism across the region.

Our Outreach Recruitment team is pleased to be attending the forum and joining these important conversations. The International Morning Plenary, moderated by BBC broadcaster Rajan Datar, connects participants around shared opportunities, new ideas and the long-term development of Mediterranean tourism.

Mediterranean Sun — Stewardship • Unity • Net Positive

This year’s theme highlights the value of responsible stewardship, regional cooperation and approaches that create a positive impact for destinations, businesses, communities and the people who power the tourism industry.

For Outreach Recruitment, the forum is a valuable opportunity to build connections, share perspectives and explore how responsible recruitment and strong people strategies can support a more sustainable and resilient Mediterranean tourism sector.

We are proud to represent Outreach Recruitment at an event that promotes collaboration, innovation and positive progress across the region.
