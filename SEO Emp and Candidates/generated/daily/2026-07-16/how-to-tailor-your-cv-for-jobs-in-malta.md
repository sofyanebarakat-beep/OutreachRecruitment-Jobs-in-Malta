# How to Tailor Your CV for Jobs in Malta

> When applying for jobs in Malta, tailoring your CV to fit local expectations and industry standards can greatly improve your chances of success. Focus on clarity, relevant experience, skills matching Maltese job roles, and including essential contact and language details to catch employers’ attention quickly.

## Key Takeaways

- Highlight relevant experience and skills aligned with Malta’s job market.
- Use a clean, professional CV format easy to scan.
- Include language skills and contact details tailored for Malta-based employers.
- Emphasize key achievements with measurable results.
- Adapt your CV for the specific role and sector in Malta.

Applying for jobs in Malta requires attention to how your CV presents your skills and professional background. ## Understanding Local CV Expectations in Malta

### Clean and Professional Layout

Maltese recruiters prefer CVs that are straightforward to read on screen or print. Use clear fonts, standard margins, and distinct section headings. Avoid overly creative designs unless applying for highly creative roles.

### Essential Sections to Include

| Section             | What to Include                                          |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Contact Information | Full name, Malta phone number (if available), email, LinkedIn |
| Personal Profile    | 2-3 sentences summarizing your experience and goals      |
| Work Experience     | Relevant roles with clear dates, responsibilities, and achievements |
| Education           | Degrees, certifications, institutions, and dates         |
| Skills              | Job-specific skills with Malta market relevance          |
| Languages          | Proficiency levels, especially English and Maltese if applicable |

---

## Practical Steps to Tailor Your CV for Maltese Jobs

### 1. Research Job Descriptions Thoroughly

Read through the job advert carefully to understand the essential skills, qualifications, and experience the employer values.

### 2. Use Keywords from the Job Posting

Incorporate keywords and phrases used in the job description to help your CV pass automated scans and appeal to recruiters.

### 3. Highlight Relevant Experience and Achievements

Focus on roles and accomplishments that match the position. Use quantifiable results where possible (e.g., "increased sales by 15%," "managed a team of 6").

### 4. Emphasize Language Skills

Malta’s official languages include English and Maltese. Fluency in English is crucial, and knowing Maltese may be advantageous depending on the sector.

### 5. Keep It Concise

Ideally, limit your CV to 2 pages. Be precise and avoid unnecessary personal information.

---

## What to Avoid in Your CV for Jobs in Malta

- Excessive personal details not relevant to the job (e.g., marital status, religion)
- Photos unless requested or common for your sector
- Including salary expectations unless explicitly asked
- Spelling and grammar mistakes—proofread carefully
- Using outdated formats like functional CVs without timelines

---

## Additional Tips for Candidates Applying in Malta

- Include a tailored cover letter linking your CV experience to the job role.
- Prepare to provide references upon request—local or international contacts with validation of your work.
- Use European CV templates like Europass only if requested; otherwise, create a custom CV aligned with Maltese business norms.
- Register your CV with trusted recruitment agencies in Malta for wider exposure.
