# How EU and Non-EU Students Can Apply to Study in Malta

> **Direct Answer:**  
> Both EU and non-EU students can apply to study in Malta by first choosing a recognised institution and course, submitting a formal application with required documents, and meeting student visa or residency requirements depending on their citizenship. EU citizens have a more straightforward process, while non-EU students must secure a student visa and proof of funds.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Malta welcomes both EU and non-EU students to its universities and colleges.  
- EU students generally do not require a visa but must register residency if staying longer than 3 months.  
- Non-EU students must apply for a student visa before arrival.  
- Applications require academic transcripts, proof of English proficiency, and financial means.  
- Malta offers quality education in various fields with English as the primary language of instruction.  
- Early application and careful preparation of documents help ensure a smooth process.

## Why Study in Malta?

Malta has rapidly become an appealing destination for international students due to its Mediterranean climate, rich history, English-speaking environment, and recognized higher education institutions. Universities in Malta offer a variety of courses across disciplines such as business, IT, engineering, health sciences, and the arts.

Studying in Malta provides:

- Access to EU educational standards and recognition  
- Opportunities to live abroad affordably compared to other Western European countries  
- A diverse international student community

## Application Process for EU Students

### Step 1: Choose Your Institution and Course  
Research Maltese universities and their offered programs. Popular options include the University of Malta and Malta College of Arts, Science and Technology (MCAST).

### Step 2: Submit Your Application  
Apply directly through the university or college's official admissions portal or via the Malta Qualifications Recognition Information Centre if coming from another EU country.

### Step 3: Provide Required Documents  
Common documents include academic transcripts, proof of language proficiency (usually IELTS or TOEFL), and identification.

### Step 4: Register Residency (for stays over 3 months)  
If planning to stay longer than three months, EU students must register for residency at the local Identity Malta office. This process requires proof of enrolment, health insurance, and financial independence.

---

## Application Process for Non-EU Students

### Step 1: Select Course and Institution  
Like EU students, begin by researching recognized institutions and selecting your course.

### Step 2: Secure an Offer of Admission  
You must obtain an official acceptance letter from the institution to proceed with a student visa application.

### Step 3: Prepare Visa Application  
Apply for a national long-stay student visa (`Type D`) through the Maltese consulate or embassy in your home country. Required documents typically include:

- Letter of acceptance  
- Academic credentials  
- Valid passport  
- Proof of accommodation and funds  
- Travel and health insurance  

### Step 4: Arrive and Register  
Upon arrival, register with Identity Malta for your student residence permit, which allows you to stay for the duration of your studies.

## Student Visa and Residency Guidance

- **EU Students:** Do not require a visa for stays under 90 days but must register residency for longer stays. Carry your European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) or equivalent.  
- **Non-EU Students:** Must obtain a student visa before travelling and apply for a residence permit upon arrival. Processing times vary; apply well in advance.  

> ⚠️ *Note:* Visa and residency policies can change. Always verify with the official [Identity Malta](https://identitymalta.com/) and Maltese consular services before applying.

## Accommodation and Working While Studying

- **Accommodation:** Options include university dormitories, private rentals, or shared flats. Prices vary, so start searching early.  
- **Employment:** Maltese regulations allow students to work part-time during the academic year and full-time during holidays, provided non-EU students check work permit conditions attached to their visa.
