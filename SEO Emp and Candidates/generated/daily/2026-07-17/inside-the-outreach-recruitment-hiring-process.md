## Understanding the Outreach Recruitment Hiring Process

Outreach Recruitment’s hiring process is built around efficiency, trust, and quality, focusing on both employer success and candidate experience. The agency carefully manages each stage to ensure the best fit for Malta’s diverse job market.

### Candidate Sourcing and Attraction

The process begins with sourcing candidates from a wide talent pool that Outreach Recruitment actively cultivates. Channels include the agency’s database, job board listings, industry-specific outreach, and referrals. The aim is to attract candidates whose skills and experience align with the roles employers seek to fill.

### Screening and Shortlisting

After sourcing, candidates undergo a detailed screening to verify qualifications, work history, and suitability for the job. This step includes reviewing CVs and conducting initial phone or video assessments to gauge motivation and compatibility.

| Step | Purpose | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| CV Review | Verify qualifications | Assess education, experience, and skills relevance |
| Initial Interview | Assess fit and interest | Evaluate candidate motivation and communication skills |
| Shortlist Creation | Select best matches | Identify candidates who meet the employer’s criteria |

### Employer Alignment and Interview Coordination

Outreach Recruitment then shares the shortlisted candidates with the employer, providing detailed profiles and insights. The agency coordinates interviews between candidates and employers, adapting to preferred formats—whether in-person or virtual.

### Feedback and Follow-up

Providing timely feedback to both parties is a key feature. Employers receive candidate evaluations and can request additional information. Candidates are given guidance about interview outcomes and next steps.

### Offer Management and Hiring Support

When an employer decides to extend an offer, Outreach Recruitment assists with negotiating terms and clarifying any queries. This support helps facilitate smooth hiring transitions and can include contract clarifications or start-date arrangements.
