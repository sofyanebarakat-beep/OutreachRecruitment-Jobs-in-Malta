## How to Search for Hospitality Jobs in Malta

### Why Choose Hospitality Jobs in Malta?

Malta’s vibrant tourism and hospitality industry makes it a popular destination for candidates seeking roles such as hotel staff, chefs, bartenders, waiters, event coordinators, and more. Seasonal peaks and year-round tourism provide a variety of employment options.

### Step 1: Explore Malta-Specific Job Boards and Websites

Start your search with dedicated local job boards and recruitment agency sites that specialise in hospitality roles. Outreach Recruitment Ltd’s [hospitality jobs hub](https://outreachrecruitment.net/hospitality-jobs-in-malta.html) lists current vacancies tailored to candidates interested in Malta’s hospitality sector.

Additional useful resources:

- Public job boards and Malta government employment portals.
- Hospitality company career pages for direct applications.
- International hospitality job platforms focusing on Mediterranean locations.

### Step 2: Register with Recruitment Agencies

Working with a recruitment agency like Outreach Recruitment Ltd can streamline your job search. Agencies understand local market demands, assist with CV preparation, and can match you with roles suited to your skills and experience.

You can [register your interest](https://outreachrecruitment.net/jobs/people-connector.html) to receive updates about suitable hospitality job openings and get personalised support.

### Step 3: Prepare a Hospitality-Focused CV and Cover Letter

Your CV should highlight relevant hospitality experience, language skills, and any certifications (e.g., food hygiene, health and safety). Tailoring your cover letter to each role and company helps demonstrate your motivation and suitability.

Refer to our [CV writing tips](https://outreachrecruitment.net/blog/cv-writing-tips-how-to-get-hired-in-malta.html) (not duplicating content here, link as a useful resource).

### Step 4: Apply Online and In Person

Submit your applications promptly through job boards, company websites, or recruitment portals. For many hospitality roles, in-person visits to hotels or restaurants may also be effective, especially during busy seasons.

### Step 5: Network and Attend Industry Events

Malta’s hospitality sector is community-driven. Attending local events, hospitality fairs, or even informal meetups can help you meet employers and secure referrals for roles not widely advertised.

### Step 6: Understand Work Eligibility and Documentation

Work eligibility in Malta depends on your nationality and permit status. EU, EEA, and Swiss citizens face fewer restrictions, while non-EU candidates should verify permit requirements. This page does not provide legal advice; candidates must consult official sources such as [Jobsplus](https://jobsplus.gov.mt) and [Identity Malta](https://identitymalta.com) for current rules.
