> Structured interviews in Malta provide a consistent, objective way for employers to evaluate candidates, reducing bias and improving the quality of hiring decisions. By asking the same job-relevant questions and scoring responses systematically, employers can better identify skilled candidates who fit their roles and company culture.

## Key Takeaways

- Structured interviews use standardized questions to ensure fairness and consistency.
- They reduce unconscious bias in candidate evaluation.
- Structured interviews improve prediction of candidate job performance.
- This approach streamlines shortlisting and decision-making.
- Employers in Malta benefit from clearer comparison of applicants.
- Structured interviews support compliance with local employment best practices.

## What Are Structured Interviews?

Structured interviews follow a set format where every candidate is asked the same questions in the same order. These questions are carefully designed to relate directly to the skills, experience, and behaviours required for the job. This contrasts with unstructured interviews, which can vary widely from one candidate to another, leading to less reliable assessments.

### Why Use Structured Interviews?

- **Consistency:** Ensures each candidate is assessed on equal footing.
- **Objectivity:** Limits personal impressions and biases from affecting decisions.
- **Job-relevance:** Focuses on competencies critical to the specific role.
- **Predictive validity:** Research supports that structured interviews better predict future job performance.

## Implementing Structured Interviews Successfully

### Preparing the Interview

- Define essential job competencies and behaviours.
- Develop a question bank linked directly to these requirements.
- Create a structured scoring guide with defined rating scales.

### Conducting the Interview

- Ask every candidate the same questions in identical order.
- Take detailed notes on each response.
- Score answers immediately to maintain objectivity.

### Post-Interview Evaluation

- Compare scores against the benchmark for each role.
- Involve multiple interviewers where possible for balanced judgments.
- Use structured interview data alongside CVs and references for final hiring decisions.

## Structured Interviews and Recruitment Agencies

This approach reduces recruitment friction and improves confidence in hiring outcomes.

## FAQs

### What is the difference between structured and unstructured interviews?

Structured interviews follow a consistent set of job-focused questions with scoring guidelines, whereas unstructured interviews are more conversational and vary between candidates, often leading to subjective assessments.

### How do structured interviews reduce bias?

By standardizing questions and scoring criteria, structured interviews limit reliance on first impressions or unconscious preferences, promoting fairer evaluations across all candidates.

### Can structured interviews be used for all job types in Malta?

Yes, structured interviews can be adapted for varied roles—from hospitality to IT or finance—by tailoring questions to the specific skills and attributes required.

### How should employers prepare for a structured interview?

Employers should identify key job competencies, develop relevant questions, and set clear scoring rubrics to ensure consistency and objectivity during interviews.

### Do structured interviews comply with Malta’s employment laws?

Using structured interviews helps meet fair hiring standards recommended in Malta and reduces risks related to discrimination claims by promoting equal treatment of all applicants.
