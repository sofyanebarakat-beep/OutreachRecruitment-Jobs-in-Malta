## What Are Structured Interviews?

Structured interviews are a systematic approach to interviewing candidates where every applicant is asked the same set of predetermined questions. These questions are designed to assess skills, experience, and behaviours relevant to the specific role. Interviewers apply uniform evaluation criteria and scoring methods to each response, allowing objective comparison across candidates.

Unlike unstructured interviews, which vary widely in style and content between interviewers or sessions, structured interviews focus on consistency and evidence-based hiring decisions.

## Benefits of Structured Interviews for Employers in Malta

### Increased Consistency and Fairness

- Every candidate is assessed using the same framework, minimising variability caused by different interviewers or question styles.  
- Fair comparison of skills and qualifications reduces risk of discrimination or favouritism.  

### Reduced Unconscious Bias

- Structured questioning limits scope for personal judgments based on unrelated factors like appearance or accent.  
- Scoring rubrics based on role-relevant criteria promote merit-based decisions.  

### Improved Predictive Validity

Research shows structured interviews better predict future job performance because they target essential competencies and past behaviours proven to correlate with success in similar roles.

### Clearer Documentation and Compliance

- Standardised notes and scores facilitate transparent decision records.  
- Employers can demonstrate adherence to Malta’s equal employment opportunity frameworks, reducing legal hiring risks.
