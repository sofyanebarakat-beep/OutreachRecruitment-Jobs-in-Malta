## Outreach Recruitment’s Matching Process

### Step 1: Employer Needs Analysis

We begin by working closely with employers to define clear job roles, skill requirements, and company culture characteristics. This stage includes:

- Gathering detailed job descriptions and must-have qualifications.
- Understanding team dynamics and workplace environment.
- Identifying short-term and long-term hiring goals.

### Step 2: Candidate Profiling

Candidates are comprehensively profiled, capturing:

- Skills and qualifications relevant to Malta’s job market.
- Work history and achievements.
- Career aspirations and personal preferences.
- Soft skills and cultural fit compatibility.

### Step 3: Screening and Shortlisting

Outreach Recruitment employs a thorough screening process to filter candidates, which includes:

- CV and application review tailored to role criteria.
- Structured interviews and competency assessments.
- Verification of qualifications and references where applicable.

This precise shortlist accelerates employer decision-making and improves the quality of interviews.

### Step 4: Interview and Feedback Coordination

We facilitate communication between candidates and employers, ensuring clarity on role expectations and candidate suitability. Feedback is collected post-interviews to refine matching and support hiring decisions.
