## Overview of the Construction Sector in Malta

The construction industry in Malta remains vital due to ongoing urban development, infrastructure renewal, and commercial projects. Construction jobs in Malta include:

- Skilled trades such as carpentry, plumbing, masonry, and painting.
- Technical roles involving site supervision and health and safety enforcement.
- Project management roles coordinating timelines, budgets, and workforce.

The sector experiences periodic fluctuations depending on public investment and private sector growth, but consistently offers employment for both local and migrant workers with necessary qualifications.

## Engineering Roles Supporting Construction

Engineering jobs in Malta support the construction sector by providing essential design, planning, and technical oversight services. Common engineering disciplines active in this market include:

- Civil Engineering: Focused on infrastructure design, environmental impact assessments, and structural projects.
- Mechanical Engineering: Overseeing mechanical systems installation and maintenance in buildings.
- Electrical Engineering: Handling electrical supply design, installations, and compliance.

Engineering roles often require recognised academic credentials and professional registrations valid in Malta or the broader EU.
