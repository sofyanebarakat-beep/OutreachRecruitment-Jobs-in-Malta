> Job references in Malta are an important part of your application, providing employers with insights into your work history and character. Prepare your references by choosing credible contacts, informing them in advance, and ensuring their details are accurate and relevant to the role you seek in Malta.

## Key Takeaways

- Select references who can speak positively about your work, skills, and reliability.  
- Inform your references before listing them in applications so they are prepared.  
- Provide up-to-date and correct contact details for each reference.  
- Understand the types of references preferred in Malta, including former employers and professional contacts.  
- Keep your reference list organized and ready to submit when requested during the hiring process.  
- Verify that your referees are willing and able to provide a strong endorsement.  

## Why Job References Matter in Malta

References support your credibility and allow prospective employers in Malta to verify your employment history, skills, and work ethics. They are often requested during or after interviews to complement your CV and application. Well-prepared references can set you apart from other candidates by providing trusted accounts of your professional capabilities.

## Choosing the Right References

### Who to Include

- Former managers or supervisors familiar with your work  
- Colleagues or team leaders who can attest to your collaboration skills  
- Clients or customers if your role involved external relationship management  
- Academic mentors or tutors for recent graduates or those changing careers  

### Who to Avoid

- Family members or friends — unless explicitly requested and relevant  
- People unfamiliar with your professional abilities or work history  

## Preparing Your References

### Contact Your References First

Before including someone's details, ask for their permission and inform them about the specific roles you are applying for in Malta. This helps ensure they are ready and willing to provide a positive and detailed recommendation.

### Share Relevant Information

Provide your referees with:

- A copy of your CV  
- Job descriptions for the roles you’re applying for  
- Key skills or achievements you would like them to highlight  

### Confirm Contact Details

Make sure you have:

| Detail          | Explanation                              |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| Full name       | Accurate spelling                       |
| Job title       | Current or most recent role held       |
| Company name    | Employer or organisation they represent|
| Phone number    | Direct number preferred                 |
| Email address   | Professional email address              |

## How to Present References in Your Application

- Include a separate references page or section if requested  
- Clearly label it and format neatly with all the requested details  
- Only provide references when asked, unless the application specifies to include them upfront  
- If not requested initially, use the wait time to prepare and update your reference list  

## Cultural Considerations for Job References in Malta

Malta employers appreciate honesty and professionalism. References should reflect your suitability and reliability without exaggeration. While personal character references may sometimes support hospitality or customer service roles, professional references remain the standard.

## FAQs

### What types of job references are common in Malta?  
Most employers in Malta expect professional references, usually from past supervisors or colleagues who can attest to your employment and skills. Academic references may be acceptable for recent graduates.

### When should I provide job references in my application?  
Only provide references upon request or if the job advertisement specifically asks for them with your initial application.

### How many references do I need for jobs in Malta?  
A typical reference list includes two to three professional contacts, but always follow specific job application instructions.

### Can I use references from outside Malta?  
Yes, international references are acceptable, especially if you are applying for roles that value previous international experience.

### What if my reference cannot be contacted?  
Inform your potential employer immediately and provide alternate contacts. It's important to keep your references updated and responsive.
