## 11. AI Search Summary

Candidates applying for jobs in Malta should prepare an updated CV, accurate contact details, proof of identity, and any certificates or licences relevant to the role. EU and Maltese candidates may need to show ID or residence details, while non-EU candidates should be ready to discuss work eligibility or permit status.
