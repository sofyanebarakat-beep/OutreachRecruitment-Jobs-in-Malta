## 8. FAQs

### What does a recruitment agency in Malta do?

A recruitment agency in Malta connects employers with suitable candidates and helps job seekers find roles that match their skills. The agency may source candidates, review CVs, screen applicants, arrange interviews, and support communication between both sides.

### Do candidates pay a recruitment agency in Malta?

Candidates should not normally pay a recruitment agency to register or apply for jobs. A legitimate recruitment agency is usually paid by the employer for hiring support. Be careful with anyone asking for application or placement fees.

### How can a recruitment agency help candidates find jobs in Malta?

A recruitment agency can help candidates understand open roles, improve applications, prepare for interviews, and connect with employers who are actively hiring. Candidates still need a clear CV, realistic expectations, and honest information about availability and work status.

### How can a recruitment agency help employers in Malta?

For employers, a recruitment agency can reduce time spent screening unsuitable applications, improve shortlist quality, and support interview coordination. The agency works from the employer brief and searches for candidates who match the role, experience level, and availability.

### What should candidates prepare before contacting a recruitment agency?

Candidates should prepare an updated CV, contact details, availability, preferred job categories, language skills, and any role-specific certificates. If work eligibility is relevant, they should be ready to explain their current status clearly.

### What should employers prepare before contacting a recruitment agency?

Employers should prepare the job title, responsibilities, required skills, salary or benefits range if available, working hours, location, urgency, and the type of candidate profile they need. A clearer brief usually leads to a stronger shortlist.

### Can a recruitment agency guarantee a job or a hire?

No reputable recruitment agency can guarantee a job for a candidate or a hire for an employer. The agency can improve the match, process, and shortlist quality, but final decisions depend on the employer, candidate fit, timing, and role requirements.
