A leading local company operating within the construction and civil works sector, delivering a wide range of projects across Malta. The organisation places strong emphasis on operational efficiency, reliability, and safety in all site and logistics activities.

Position Summary

The Driver (Licence B) is responsible for the safe and timely transportation of materials, tools, and personnel between company sites, suppliers, and project locations. The role supports day-to-day operations by ensuring smooth logistics and reliable delivery services.

Key Responsibilities

- Transport materials, tools, and equipment to and from construction sites and warehouses.
- Deliver and collect documents, supplies, and other company items as required.
- Support loading and unloading of materials in a safe and organised manner.
- Carry out routine vehicle checks and report any maintenance or safety issues.
- Ensure company vehicles are kept clean, roadworthy, and compliant with regulations.
- Follow planned routes and schedules while adapting to operational needs when required.
- Assist site teams with basic manual tasks when not engaged in driving duties.
- Maintain accurate records of deliveries, mileage, and fuel usage where required.
- Adhere strictly to road safety laws and company transport policies.

Requirements

- Valid Category B driving licence (mandatory).
- Previous experience in a driving or logistics role is considered an advantage.
- Good knowledge of Maltese roads and traffic regulations.
- Responsible, punctual, and safety-conscious attitude.
- Ability to handle physical tasks such as loading and unloading materials.
- Basic communication skills and ability to follow instructions.
- Flexible and willing to support operational needs as required.

What We Offer

- Stable full-time employment within a reputable operational environment.
- Opportunity to work across varied sites and projects.
- Supportive team environment with clear operational structure.
- On-the-job exposure to logistics and construction operations.
