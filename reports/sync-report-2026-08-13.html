Our client is a leading hospitality group bringing together five unique restaurants in Malta, each offering its own distinctive dining experience while sharing a passion for genuine Italian cuisine, exceptional food, and warm hospitality.

Position Summary

Our client is looking for a friendly and customer-focused Deli Counter Assistant / Salumeria Specialist to join their team. The successful candidate will be responsible for serving customers, preparing and presenting a selection of quality Italian deli products, and ensuring an excellent customer experience.

Key Responsibilities

- Serve customers in a friendly, professional, and efficient manner.
- Prepare and serve a selection of Italian cold cuts, cheeses, antipasti, and other deli products.
- Slice and portion products accurately according to customer requirements.
- Prepare deli boards, platters, sandwiches, and other food items as required.
- Provide customers with information and recommendations regarding products.
- Maintain high standards of product presentation and quality.
- Ensure all food products are stored, labelled, and handled correctly.
- Monitor stock levels and communicate replenishment requirements.
- Maintain a clean, organised, and hygienic deli counter and work area.
- Follow all food safety, hygiene, and health and safety regulations.
- Operate the point of sale system and process customer orders accurately.
- Assist with receiving, checking, and storing deliveries when required.
- Work closely with the wider restaurant team to ensure smooth daily operations.

Requirements

- Must currently reside in Malta.
- Previous experience in a deli, salumeria, food retail, restaurant, or similar hospitality environment will be considered an advantage.
- Knowledge of Italian cold cuts, cheeses, antipasti, and other deli products is an advantage.
- Excellent customer service and communication skills.
- Fluent in English; knowledge of Italian or other languages will be considered an advantage.
- Friendly, professional, and customer-focused attitude.
- Good attention to detail and strong organisational skills.
- Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
- Good knowledge of food hygiene and safety standards.
- Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
- Flexibility to work shifts, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.

What We Offer

- Opportunity to work within a growing and dynamic hospitality group.
- Professional and supportive working environment.
- Training and development opportunities.
- Opportunities for career progression.
- The chance to work with high-quality Italian food products and a passionate hospitality team.
