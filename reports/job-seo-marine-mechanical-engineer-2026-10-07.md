### About the Role
Our client, a growing Mediterranean aquaculture business, is seeking an experienced Marine Mechanical Engineer to take responsibility for the technical performance and mechanical reliability of its fleet of approximately 35 vessels operating across the Mediterranean.
This is primarily a technical, planning, maintenance and procurement role. You will run the planned maintenance system, act as the technical reference for engines, propulsion and auxiliary machinery, source and buy parts and services, manage critical spares and budgets, and work closely with vessel crews, mechanics, contractors and suppliers. The salary is €43,000–€47,000 per year, based on experience.
It suits a marine or mechanical engineer with a degree or MCAST diploma (MQF Level 5 or above), at least three years in marine engineering, fleet maintenance or a technical superintendent role, and a full driving licence.

### Key Responsibilities
- Develop and manage planned maintenance systems for the fleet, including schedules, work orders, running hours and service history
- Work closely with the Fleet Superintendent to coordinate maintenance carried out by in-house mechanics and external contractors
- Act as the technical reference for main engines, gearboxes, propulsion systems, generators, pumps, compressors, hydraulics and deck machinery
- Plan maintenance according to the operational calendar so vessels are available when required
- Identify required parts using technical manuals, parts catalogues, engine serial numbers and OEM documentation
- Source and purchase parts and technical services from local and international suppliers
- Request and compare quotations based on price, quality and lead time
- Raise purchase orders and follow up on deliveries, imports, returns and warranty claims
- Develop and maintain a network of approved suppliers and service providers
- Manage critical spare parts and keep appropriate stock levels to minimise vessel downtime
- Monitor maintenance and spare parts budgets and track costs across the fleet
- Maintain accurate technical documentation, manuals, certificates, inspection records and purchase history
- Recommend upgrades, replacements and refits to improve reliability, efficiency and long-term operating costs

### Requirements
- Degree or MCAST Diploma in Marine Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline at MQF Level 5 or above
- At least 3 years of experience in marine engineering, fleet maintenance or a technical superintendent role
- Strong technical knowledge of marine diesel engines, propulsion systems, hydraulics and auxiliary equipment
- Experience in maintenance planning and the procurement of technical parts and services
- Full driving licence
- Good spoken and written English

### What's on Offer
- Salary of €43,000–€47,000 per year, based on experience
- The opportunity to work with a technically varied fleet
- A key technical role within a growing Mediterranean aquaculture business
- Regional exposure through the wider group network

### Skills Required
- Planned maintenance systems and work orders
- Marine diesel engines, propulsion, hydraulics and auxiliary equipment
- Analytical and fault-finding skills
- Technical procurement, quotations and purchase orders
- Commercial awareness and negotiation
- Accurate handling of part numbers, specifications and technical records
- Communication with crews, mechanics, contractors, suppliers and management

### Languages
- English – good, spoken and written (required)
- Maltese – an advantage
