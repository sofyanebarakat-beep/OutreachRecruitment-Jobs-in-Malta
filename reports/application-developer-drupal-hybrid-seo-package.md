## Section 4 - Job Content

<h2>Application Developer (Drupal) - Hybrid</h2>

<p>Outreach Recruitment Ltd is recruiting an experienced Application Developer with strong Drupal 10 or newer expertise for a full-time hybrid IT opportunity based around Valletta, Malta. This role is suited to a senior developer who can support, extend and maintain complex Drupal websites and applications in an international technology environment.</p>

<h2>About the Role</h2>

<p>The successful candidate will work as part of a specialist team cooperating closely with a major client IT team. The role focuses on Drupal website management, custom development, application support, integrations, migrations and ongoing maintenance across enterprise-level information systems.</p>

<h2>Key Responsibilities</h2>

- Install, support and perform security-related upgrades for Drupal websites.
- Develop custom Drupal modules, plugins, templates and other functionality.
- Maintain the logical and physical structure of Drupal websites, pages and navigation.
- Support applications during the maintenance phase and deliver new custom features based on business needs.
- Integrate Drupal systems with other Drupal environments and third-party applications, platforms or services.
- Customize and extend Drupal themes.
- Migrate data from SharePoint Online and SharePoint 2019 to Drupal.
- Administer, configure, support and maintain applications and underlying infrastructure.

<h2>Requirements</h2>

- University degree.
- At least 10 years of professional IT experience.
- Experience working with Drupal 10 or newer.
- Experience with Drupal content structuring, storage, access and presentation.
- Strong knowledge of Drupal hooks, APIs, module development, theming and content moderation modules.
- Experience creating Drupal 10 or newer web applications from scratch.
- Experience designing and developing responsive web applications.
- Object-oriented programming experience.
- SharePoint Online to Drupal and SharePoint 2019 to Drupal migration experience.
- MySQL database experience, including cloud equivalents such as Azure Database for MySQL.
- Experience with reporting and visualization libraries such as D3.js or Chart.js.
- Knowledge of data analysis techniques and KPIs.
- Web technology experience including JavaScript, XML, PHP, HTML5, CSS3 and AJAX.
- JavaScript, jQuery and front-end framework experience such as React, Angular or Vue.js.
- Experience with Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft Graph API and Azure DevOps.
- Experience developing and consuming web services and APIs.
- Excellent command of English.

<h2>Preferred Qualifications</h2>

- Enterprise Drupal project experience.
- Multilingual Drupal site deployment experience.
- Experience working with major institutional or international technology environments.

<h2>Benefits</h2>

<h2>Why Join This Company</h2>
