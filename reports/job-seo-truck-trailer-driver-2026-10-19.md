### About the Role
Our client is looking for a reliable and responsible Truck / Trailer Driver to join their transport and logistics operations in Marsa, Malta. The successful candidate will be responsible for transporting goods, materials, or equipment safely and efficiently while following road traffic regulations and company procedures.
Day to day, you will transport goods, materials or equipment to designated locations, complete deliveries and collections on schedule, inspect the vehicle before and after journeys, ensure loads are properly secured, follow designated routes and road traffic regulations, and maintain accurate delivery records and transport documentation.
It suits a safety-conscious, punctual driver who holds a valid licence for the relevant vehicle category and is currently residing in Malta. Previous experience driving trucks and/or trailers is preferred but not mandatory.

### Key Responsibilities
- Transport goods, materials, or equipment to designated locations
- Complete deliveries and collections according to the assigned schedule
- Inspect the vehicle before and after journeys and report any defects
- Ensure loads are properly secured and transported safely
- Follow designated routes, delivery schedules, and road traffic regulations
- Maintain accurate delivery records and complete transport documentation
- Keep the vehicle clean and in good working condition
- Communicate with supervisors regarding delays or operational issues
- Follow health and safety procedures and report accidents or incidents immediately
- Perform other transport-related duties as required

### Requirements
- Must currently be residing in Malta
- Previous experience driving trucks and/or trailers is preferred
- A valid driving licence for the relevant vehicle category is mandatory
- A valid Maltese driving licence is considered an advantage; foreign licences may be accepted subject to applicable Maltese licensing and recognition requirements
- Valid Driver CPC and other professional driving authorisations, where legally required
- Good knowledge of road safety and traffic regulations
- Responsible driving habits and a safety-conscious attitude
- Basic communication skills in English
- Ability to follow instructions and maintain accurate records
- Reliable, punctual, and flexible to meet operational requirements
- Must be legally authorised to work in Malta

### What's on Offer
- Full-time position with a basic salary
- Possibility of a salary increment following the probation period
- Any salary adjustment is subject to performance evaluation and the company's assessment
- Opportunity to join an established transport and logistics operation in Marsa

### Skills Required
- Safe vehicle inspection and defect reporting
- Load securing and safe transport practices
- Route planning and delivery schedule adherence
- Delivery and transport documentation
- Road safety and traffic regulation compliance
- Basic communication in English
