Our client, a well-established company specialising in tower cranes, is looking to recruit a Crane Installer to join its team in Malta. The company is based in Mqabba and the work takes place at various construction sites.
You will install, assemble and dismantle cranes, help relocate them when required and support maintenance. That means using hand and power tools safely, inspecting components before installation and reporting any technical issues.
