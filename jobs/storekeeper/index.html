Storekeeper

Join the warehouse and logistics team of a leading home retail company in Birkirkara and help keep stock moving safely, accurately and efficiently.

Full-Time PositionTraining Provided
Apply Now

Are you organised, reliable and comfortable in a busy warehouse? This Storekeeper job in Birkirkara is suited to someone who takes pride in accurate stock handling, safe working practices and helping colleagues and customers.

About the Role

Our client is a leading retail company in Malta offering a wide range of products and solutions for the home. The Storekeeper will support the efficient receipt, storage and distribution of stock across its retail operations.

You will receive, store, handle and dispatch goods, maintain accurate inventory records and prepare items for store replenishment and customer collections. The work is active and hands-on, with a strong focus on safety, organisation and accuracy.

Key Responsibilities

Goods Receiving and Dispatch

  • Load and unload stock safely and efficiently.
  • Inspect received goods and report discrepancies, damage or shortages.
  • Record incoming and outgoing goods according to company procedures.
  • Prepare and release customer collections accurately and efficiently.

Stock Handling and Inventory Control

  • Store products in an orderly, accessible and clearly labelled way.
  • Allocate stock to the correct storage areas.
  • Update the inventory management system for all goods received and issued.
  • Assist with stock takes and periodic inventory counts.
  • Report stock discrepancies or irregularities promptly.

Safety, Service and Teamwork

  • Maintain a clean, organised and safe warehouse.
  • Use safe lifting and manual-handling techniques.
  • Assist retail staff with stock requests and fulfil customer orders accurately.
  • Support colleagues with daily warehouse operations.
  • Participate in training programmes and complete other reasonable duties assigned by the Supervisor.

Key Requirements

  • Previous warehouse, logistics, stockroom or storekeeping experience is an advantage.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to handle stock accurately and responsibly.
  • Physical fitness for regular manual handling and safe lifting.
  • A reliable, responsible and cooperative approach.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Flexibility for shifts, including weekends and public holidays when required.

Skills Required

  • Inventory management and accurate record keeping
  • Goods receiving, inspection and dispatch
  • Stock labelling, allocation and stocktaking
  • Safe lifting and manual handling
  • Warehouse organisation and housekeeping
  • Customer and internal service

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with an inventory management system and previous work in a retail warehouse, logistics operation or stockroom will be useful. No specific certification or licence was stated by the employer.

What We Offer

Training programmes are available to improve operational knowledge and efficiency, together with the opportunity to contribute to the daily operations of a leading retail business in Malta.

About the Company

Our client is a leading retail company in Malta offering a wide selection of products and solutions for the home. The business depends on an organised warehouse team to keep stores replenished and customer orders moving.

Reporting To

The Storekeeper reports to the Supervisor.

Location

The role is based on-site in Birkirkara, a central Maltese town with convenient connections to surrounding retail and commercial areas.
