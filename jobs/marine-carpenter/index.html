Marine Carpenter — Biot, France

Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking a Marine Carpenter for a full-time, on-site role based in Biot, France. This position is part of a skilled carpentry team within a leading international marine group specialising in ship repair, yacht refit, and marine engineering services.

About the Role

The ideal candidate brings strong expertise in teak deck fabrication, bespoke joinery, and precision woodworking to high-end superyacht and yacht refit standards. You will work on prestigious vessel projects delivering crafted carpentry solutions for marine interiors and exterior deck installations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Fabrication and installation of wooden fixtures, trims, decking, and decorative marine elements.
  • Precision cutting, shaping, and fitting of marine-grade woods and materials.
  • Design, craft, and refurbish bespoke joinery solutions for cabins, galleys, salons, and entertainment areas.
  • Restore and upgrade existing woodwork to high-end yacht and superyacht standards.
  • Installation, caulking, sanding, and finishing of teak decks with precise alignment and high-quality finishing.
  • Remove and replace worn decking with precise alignment standards.
  • Select and use marine-grade, moisture-resistant woods and finishing products.
  • Apply coatings, sealants, and protective finishes to ensure durability in marine environments.
  • Ensure all work meets strict quality and aesthetic standards.
  • Provide carpentry services for hospitality, architectural, and commercial projects requiring premium woodwork.

Requirements

  • Minimum 3 years of experience in marine carpentry, yacht refit, or luxury woodworking.
  • Strong expertise in teak deck fabrication and installation.
  • Proficiency with hand tools, power tools, and CNC machinery.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and templates.
  • Strong attention to detail with a commitment to high-quality finishing standards.
  • Good teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience on superyacht or luxury yacht interior refit projects.
  • Formal carpentry or joinery qualification.
  • Familiarity with marine-grade coating and sealant systems.

Benefits

  • Full-time employment with a leading international marine group.
  • Competitive remuneration package based on experience and skillset.
  • Opportunity to work on prestigious yacht and superyacht projects.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Collaborative and innovation-driven working environment.

Why Join This Company

This vacancy offers a full-time opportunity within a prestigious international marine group in France. Candidates will build specialist woodworking expertise, work on prestigious vessel projects, and develop their marine carpentry career within an experienced and professional team.
