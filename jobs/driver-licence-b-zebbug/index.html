Driver (Licence B) — Żebbuġ, Malta

If you hold a valid Category B driving licence and are looking for stable, full-time work with a well-established construction company in Malta, this could be the right move for you. Based in Żebbuġ with routes across the island, this role offers consistent hours, a structured team environment, and on-the-job training — ideal for a reliable driver who takes pride in doing the job right, every day.

About the Role

Our client is a leading construction and civil works company operating across multiple sites in Malta. They are seeking a dependable Driver (Licence B) to join their team and support the smooth day-to-day movement of materials, tools, and personnel between construction sites and company warehouses.

This is a hands-on, active role that goes beyond simply driving. You will be part of the site support team, assisting with loading and unloading, keeping accurate delivery records, and ensuring vehicles are maintained to a high standard. The successful candidate will be someone who takes responsibility seriously and can be counted on to get the job done safely and on time.

With exposure to varied construction projects across Malta, no two days will look exactly the same. If you want a role where you stay active, work alongside a committed team, and develop your logistics skills in a growing sector, this is an excellent opportunity.

Key Responsibilities

  • Transport materials, tools, and equipment safely to construction sites and warehouses across Malta
  • Deliver and collect documents, supplies, and materials as directed
  • Support loading and unloading operations, following safe handling procedures
  • Conduct routine pre-trip and post-trip vehicle maintenance checks and report any defects promptly
  • Maintain vehicle cleanliness and ensure compliance with road and company regulations
  • Follow planned routes and adapt efficiently to changing operational needs
  • Assist site teams with manual tasks when available
  • Accurately record all deliveries, mileage, and fuel usage
  • Comply with Maltese road safety laws and all company health and safety policies

Key Requirements

  • Valid Category B driving licence — mandatory
  • Knowledge of Maltese roads and traffic regulations
  • Responsible, punctual, and safety-conscious approach to work
  • Physically capable of supporting loading and unloading tasks
  • Good communication skills and ability to follow instructions
  • Flexibility to meet operational demands when needed

Skills Required

  • Valid Category B driving licence
  • Pre-trip and post-trip vehicle inspection
  • Route planning and time management
  • Mileage and fuel log record keeping
  • Manual handling and safe load securing
  • Knowledge of Maltese road network

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience as a driver in a logistics or construction environment
  • Experience supporting site teams or working within a construction setting

What We Offer

  • Stable, full-time employment with a reputable construction company
  • Competitive salary based on experience
  • Exposure to varied construction projects and sites across Malta
  • On-the-job logistics and operations training
  • Supportive team environment with clear structure and responsibilities

About the Company

Our client is a well-established construction and civil works company with active projects across multiple locations in Malta. Known for their commitment to quality and safety, they offer a structured working environment where each team member plays a key role in delivering results on the ground. They are looking for reliable professionals who take pride in their work and are ready to contribute from day one.
