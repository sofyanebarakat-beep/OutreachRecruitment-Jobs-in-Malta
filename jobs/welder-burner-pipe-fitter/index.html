Welder / Burner / Pipe Fitter — Żejtun, Malta

Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking a Welder / Burner / Pipe Fitter for a full-time, on-site marine role based in Żejtun, Malta. This position is with a leading international marine group specialising in ship repair, yacht refit, and marine engineering services, operating modern shipyard facilities in Malta with a strong focus on quality, safety, and precision.

About the Role

The Welder / Burner / Pipe Fitter is responsible for welding, cutting, fabrication, and pipe fitting works on steel, aluminium, stainless steel, and other materials within a shipyard environment. The role includes fabricating, assembling, installing, repairing, and testing metal structures and pipe systems in line with technical drawings, welding procedures, safety regulations, and quality standards. Strong aluminium welding and fabrication experience is highly valued.

Key Responsibilities

  • Carry out welding, burning/cutting, fabrication, and pipe fitting repairs on structural components and pipework systems.
  • Measure, cut, shape, fit, and assemble metal components for structural and pipework applications.
  • Fabricate and repair steel, aluminium, and stainless-steel parts, pipe spools, and assemblies.
  • Read and interpret engineering drawings, isometrics, sketches, and work instructions.
  • Prepare materials through cutting, bevelling, grinding, alignment, and tack welding.
  • Perform MIG, TIG, and MMA welding according to approved procedures.
  • Operate welding, oxy-fuel, plasma cutting, and related equipment safely and effectively.
  • Install and repair pipe systems including valves, flanges, bends, supports, and related components.
  • Assist with pressure testing, leak testing, and quality inspections, including defect rectification.
  • Follow all welding procedures, pipe fitting standards, and health & safety regulations.
  • Maintain clean, safe, and organised work areas and equipment.
  • Work collaboratively with supervisors, engineers, and other trades to meet project deadlines.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Welder, Burner, Fabricator, and/or Pipe Fitter in shipyard, marine, or heavy industrial environments.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical and fabrication drawings, including isometrics.
  • Strong aluminium welding and fabrication experience is essential.
  • Good knowledge of steel, stainless steel, and aluminium materials and processes.
  • Practical pipe fitting experience, including installation and alignment of pipe systems.
  • Proficiency in MIG, TIG, and MMA welding.
  • Welding certification required; pipe fitting qualifications considered an asset.
  • Experience with oxy-fuel and plasma cutting is an advantage.
  • Ability to work safely in shipyard conditions, including confined spaces and at height.
  • Strong commitment to health & safety and quality standards.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Welding certification (MIG, TIG, MMA) from a recognised body.
  • Pipe fitting qualifications or trade certification.
  • Experience with oxy-fuel and plasma cutting equipment.
  • Previous work on prestige yacht refit or ship repair projects.

Benefits

  • Full-time employment with a leading international marine group in Malta.
  • Competitive remuneration package based on experience and qualifications.
  • Work on challenging and prestigious marine projects in a professional shipyard environment.
  • Team that values craftsmanship, safety, and excellence.
