Shipyard Sprayer / Painter — Żejtun, Malta

Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking a Shipyard Sprayer / Painter for a full-time, on-site marine role based in Żejtun, Malta. This position supports a leading international marine group, covering protective coating application, surface preparation, and high-quality marine finishing on yachts, superyachts, and commercial vessels.

About the Role

The ideal candidate brings hands-on experience in marine surface finishing, teak deck preparation, and protective coatings application. You will work as part of a skilled shipyard team delivering precise, high-standard finishing work on prestigious marine projects in Malta.

Key Responsibilities

  • Apply coatings, sealants, and protective finishes to ensure durability in marine environments.
  • Fabricate and install wooden fixtures, trims, decking, and decorative marine elements.
  • Carry out precision cutting, shaping, and fitting of marine-grade woods and materials.
  • Install, caulk, sand, and finish teak decks to exacting standards with precise alignment.
  • Remove and replace worn decking with high-quality surface finishing.
  • Design, craft, and refurbish bespoke joinery solutions for cabins, galleys, and salon areas.
  • Restore and upgrade existing woodwork and surfaces to superyacht standards.
  • Select and use marine-grade, moisture-resistant materials and finishing products.
  • Ensure all work meets strict quality, safety, and aesthetic standards.
  • Support hospitality and commercial projects requiring premium marine-grade finishing.

Requirements

  • Minimum 3 years of experience in marine surface finishing, yacht refit, or shipyard painting.
  • Strong expertise in teak deck preparation, caulking, and protective coating application.
  • Proficiency with spray equipment, surface preparation tools, hand tools, and power tools.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and specifications.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to high-quality finishing standards.
  • Good teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience on superyacht or luxury vessel refit projects.
  • Knowledge of marine-grade coating systems and protective sealant products.
  • CNC machinery proficiency for precision cutting and shaping operations.

Benefits

  • Full-time employment with a leading international marine group.
  • Competitive remuneration package based on experience and skillset.
  • Opportunity to work on prestigious yacht and superyacht projects.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Collaborative and innovation-driven working environment.

Why Join This Company

This vacancy offers a full-time opportunity within a leading international marine group in Malta. Candidates will build specialist finishing expertise, contribute to prestigious yacht refit projects, and work alongside experienced shipyard professionals in a high-standard marine environment.
