About the Role

The successful candidate will work as part of a specialist team cooperating closely with a major client IT team. The role focuses on Drupal website management, custom development, application support, integrations, migrations and ongoing maintenance across enterprise-level information systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Install, support and perform security-related upgrades for Drupal websites.
  • Develop custom Drupal modules, plugins, templates and other custom functionality.
  • Maintain the logical and physical structure of Drupal websites, pages and navigation.
  • Support applications during maintenance and deliver new custom features based on business needs.
  • Integrate Drupal systems with other Drupal environments and third-party applications, platforms or services.
  • Customize and extend Drupal themes.
  • Migrate data from SharePoint Online and SharePoint 2019 to Drupal.
  • Administer, configure, support and maintain applications and underlying infrastructure.

Requirements

  • University degree and at least 10 years of professional IT experience.
  • Experience working with Drupal 10 or newer.
  • Experience with Drupal content structuring, storage, access and presentation.
  • Strong knowledge of Drupal hooks, APIs, module development, theming and content moderation modules.
  • Experience creating Drupal 10 or newer web applications from scratch.
  • Experience designing responsive web applications and working with object-oriented programming languages.
  • Experience migrating from SharePoint Online and SharePoint 2019 to Drupal.
  • MySQL experience, including cloud equivalents such as Azure Database for MySQL.
  • Experience with reporting and visualization libraries such as D3.js or Chart.js.
  • Experience with JavaScript, XML, PHP, HTML5, CSS3, AJAX, jQuery and frameworks such as React, Angular or Vue.js.
  • Experience with Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft Graph API and Azure DevOps.
  • Experience developing and consuming web services and APIs.
  • Excellent command of English.
