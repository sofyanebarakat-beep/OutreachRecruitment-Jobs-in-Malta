Electronic Technician — Biot, France

Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking an Electronic Technician for a full-time, on-site role based in Biot, France. This position supports technical engineering activities for a leading international marine group, covering electronic system maintenance, diagnostics, calibration, and installation across marine and high-performance engine platforms.

About the Role

The ideal candidate brings hands-on experience with electronic diagnostics, control cable wiring, engine electronic systems, and technical documentation. The role may involve international travel for training and on-site field service assignments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform technical functions supporting engineering activities, including setup, operation, maintenance, modification, testing, calibration, and troubleshooting of electronic systems.
  • Provide customer support including electronic installations and routine maintenance activities.
  • Read and interpret blueprints, schematics, and technical diagrams.
  • Install, connect, and terminate control cables and wiring systems.
  • Diagnose electronic faults using tools such as voltmeters, cable testers, and thermal scanners.
  • Adjust, repair, and replace defective electronic components using hand and power tools.
  • Prepare technical sketches and follow schematics for wiring and equipment installation.
  • Maintain accurate documentation and prepare technical reports on findings and interventions.
  • Service and maintain engine electronic systems, ensuring operational reliability.
  • Travel internationally for training and on-site service support when required.
  • Support team operations and carry out additional tasks as assigned.

Requirements

  • Technical diploma or associate degree in Electronics, Electrical Engineering Technology, or related field.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience as an Electronic or Electrical Technician.
  • Experience with MTU engines or comparable high-performance systems is advantageous.
  • Proficiency with diagnostic tools, soldering, and electronic rework techniques.
  • Ability to read and interpret electronic schematics and technical manuals.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Good communication and documentation skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with MTU or other marine diesel engine electronics.
  • Field service or customer-facing technical support background.
  • Willingness and flexibility to travel internationally for service assignments.

Benefits

  • Full-time employment with a leading international marine group.
  • Competitive compensation based on experience and qualifications.
  • International travel and training opportunities.
  • Career development within a specialised marine engineering field.
  • Collaborative and professional working environment.

Why Join This Company

This vacancy offers a full-time opportunity within a leading marine engineering group in France, working on high-performance marine electronic systems. Candidates will gain specialist technical experience, access international training, and grow their career in a demanding and rewarding marine technical environment.
