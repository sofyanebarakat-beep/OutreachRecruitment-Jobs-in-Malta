## Section 4 — Job Content

### Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking an experienced Marine Mechanical Foreman for a full-time, on-site position in Paola, South Eastern Malta. This role is placed on behalf of a well-established organisation within the marine engineering and ship repair sector. The successful candidate will take charge of coordinating and supervising mechanical operations onboard vessels and within shipyard facilities, with a strong focus on repair, maintenance, and refit projects.

This is a hands-on leadership position that requires proven mechanical expertise and a track record of supervising teams in a shipyard or large-scale industrial environment. If you have a strong background in marine mechanical maintenance, ship repair, and dry-docking operations, this Marine Mechanical Foreman vacancy in Malta offers the opportunity to take on a meaningful role in a technically demanding and rewarding sector.

### About the Role

The Marine Mechanical Foreman will be responsible for the coordination and supervision of mechanical activities across a range of ship repair, maintenance, and refit projects in Paola, Malta. Working closely with project managers, clients, surveyors, and classification societies, the Foreman ensures that all mechanical works are completed safely, efficiently, and in line with technical specifications, quality standards, and agreed timelines.

This position is central to the smooth running of mechanical operations within the shipyard — from managing tools and workforce to overseeing the maintenance and overhaul of engines, pumps, compressors, valves, and auxiliary systems. The role is suited to a detail-oriented, technically skilled professional who can lead teams with confidence under tight operational deadlines.

### Key Responsibilities

**Supervision & Coordination**
- Coordinate and supervise mechanical repair, maintenance, and installation activities on vessels and within the shipyard.
- Lead teams of mechanics, fitters, and specialised technicians across assigned projects.
- Monitor work progress and ensure compliance with project schedules and milestones.
- Manage tools, equipment, and workforce assigned to mechanical operations.

**Maintenance & Overhaul**
- Supervise the maintenance, overhaul, repair, and replacement of mechanical equipment and shipboard systems.
- Coordinate activities involving engines, pumps, compressors, valves, auxiliary systems, and other machinery.
- Ensure compliance with technical procedures, work instructions, and quality requirements.

**Project Support**
- Support the planning and execution of dry-docking and refit activities.
- Coordinate with clients, surveyors, classification societies, and project teams as required.

**Health, Safety & Environment**
- Promote and enforce health, safety, and environmental standards across all works.
- Ensure all team activities comply with shipyard safety regulations and industry standards.

### Requirements

- Proven experience in marine mechanical maintenance, ship repair, or a similar industrial environment.
- Previous supervisory experience in a shipyard or large-scale mechanical operation.
- Strong knowledge of marine machinery and shipboard mechanical systems.
- Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and mechanical documentation.
- Experience with ship repair, refit, and dry-docking projects.
- Good understanding of shipyard safety regulations and industry standards.
- Strong leadership, communication, and organisational skills.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver results under tight deadlines.
- Fluent English — written and spoken.

### Preferred Qualifications

- Relevant technical or trade qualification in mechanical engineering or a related marine discipline.
- Familiarity with classification society requirements and survey processes.
- Experience working with international crews or multinational project teams.

### What We Offer

- Full-time, stable employment with a reputable marine engineering organisation in Malta.
- Exposure to complex ship repair, maintenance, and dry-docking projects.
- Professional and supportive working environment.
- Career development and progression opportunities.
- Opportunity to work alongside experienced maritime engineering teams.
