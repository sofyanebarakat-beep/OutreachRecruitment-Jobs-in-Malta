### Introduction

Outreach Recruitment is seeking a highly organised and analytical Administration Specialist for a full-time, on-site position in Floriana, Malta. This vacancy sits within a dynamic healthcare and insurance operations environment and offers the opportunity to play a key role in overseeing medical claims processes, cost containment initiatives, case management activities, and governance and compliance standards.

This Administration Specialist role in Malta is suited to a detail-oriented professional who can balance operational efficiency, financial stewardship, and exceptional service delivery. If you have a background in insurance administration, healthcare operations, or medical claims management, and are looking for a meaningful role with genuine career development potential, we would like to hear from you.

### About the Role

The Administration Specialist will be responsible for overseeing and optimising administrative processes across healthcare and insurance operations in Floriana, Malta. Working closely with assistance, claims, medical, and provider network teams, the successful candidate will ensure smooth day-to-day operations while maintaining full compliance with internal policies, regulatory requirements, and data protection standards.

This is a broad and varied position that spans vendor invoice processing via SAP, management reporting, data analysis, and support for cost containment and case management functions. The role requires someone who is equally comfortable managing detailed documentation and contributing to strategic operational improvements.

### Key Responsibilities

**Administrative Operations**
- Oversee and optimise administrative processes related to medical claims and healthcare operations.
- Ensure compliance with internal policies, regulatory requirements, and data protection standards.
- Coordinate with assistance, claims, medical, and provider network teams to ensure smooth operations.
- Process vendor invoices and payments through SAP and perform payment runs accurately and within deadlines.
- Prepare reports, collect and analyse data, and provide administrative support to management.
- Maintain accurate digital and paper-based records, files, and archives.
- Contribute to the development and maintenance of process documentation and operational manuals.

**Cost Containment & Claims Management**
- Support the implementation of cost containment strategies aimed at reducing claim costs while maintaining quality of care.
- Monitor billing practices and identify discrepancies, duplicate charges, or non-covered services.
- Collaborate with provider networks to support cost-effective healthcare solutions.
- Assist in reviewing high-cost treatments and healthcare utilisation trends.
- Support initiatives focused on healthcare cost optimisation and resource management.

**Case Management Support**
- Assist in monitoring and coordinating case management activities.
- Support the identification and management of complex or high-cost cases.
- Help ensure effective care coordination and positive customer outcomes.
- Promote efficient and cost-effective care pathways where appropriate.

**Data Analysis & Reporting**
- Analyse operational and claims data to identify trends and improvement opportunities.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and prepare management reports.
- Support data-driven decision-making through accurate reporting and analysis.
- Assist with fraud, waste, and abuse detection through audits and data reviews.

**Governance & Compliance**
- Ensure adherence to company procedures, healthcare regulations, and data protection requirements.
- Maintain accurate documentation to support regulatory and legal compliance.
- Support internal audits and continuous improvement initiatives.

### Requirements

- Degree in Healthcare Administration, Business Administration, Nursing, or a related field.
- Previous experience within insurance, healthcare administration, medical claims, TPA services, or a similar environment.
- Familiarity with healthcare systems, medical claims processes, and cost containment practices.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent organisational and multitasking abilities.
- Ability to work independently and manage priorities effectively.
- High attention to detail and accuracy.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

### Preferred Qualifications

- Knowledge of Maltese considered an asset.
- Experience with SAP or similar ERP systems considered an advantage.
- Strong analytical and strategic mindset.
- Ability to balance operational efficiency with customer and patient outcomes.
- Proactive approach to continuous improvement.
- High level of integrity, professionalism, and confidentiality.

### What We Offer

- Competitive remuneration package.
- Hybrid working opportunities.
- Professional development and training opportunities.
- Exposure to international healthcare and insurance operations.
- Opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects that positively impact customer and patient outcomes.
