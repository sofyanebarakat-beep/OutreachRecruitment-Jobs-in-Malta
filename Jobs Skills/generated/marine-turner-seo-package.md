### Introduction

A Marine Turner vacancy is now open in Paola, Malta through Outreach Recruitment Ltd. This is a full-time, on-site machining role within the marine engineering and ship repair sector — one of Malta's most established industrial industries.

The successful candidate will operate conventional and CNC lathes to produce, repair, and refurbish precision mechanical components for ship repair, vessel refit, and overhaul projects. If you are an experienced turner or machinist with a background in marine or heavy engineering, this is your opportunity to apply your skills in a dynamic shipyard environment in Malta.

---

### About the Role

As a Marine Turner, you will be central to the workshop's precision machining operations. Your work will directly support ship repair and vessel refit projects by producing and restoring components to exact technical tolerances. You will work from engineering drawings, operating both conventional and CNC lathes, and carry out dimensional inspections using precision measuring instruments.

The role is primarily workshop-based but may occasionally require work onboard vessels. You will collaborate with maintenance and repair teams during active refit and overhaul activities, contributing to the delivery of high-quality machining work within shipyard timelines and quality standards.

---

### Key Responsibilities

- Perform turning and machining operations as part of ship repair and vessel refit projects
- Repair, refurbish, and manufacture components including shafts, bushings, flanges, sleeves, bearing housings, and related mechanical parts
- Operate conventional and CNC lathes to produce precision-machined parts to required specifications
- Read and interpret engineering drawings, technical documentation, and machining instructions
- Carry out dimensional inspections using precision measuring instruments such as micrometers, vernier calipers, and bore gauges
- Restore worn or damaged components to required tolerances and specifications
- Support maintenance and repair teams during vessel refit and overhaul activities
- Perform machining work in the workshop and, when required, onboard vessels in port
- Comply with quality standards and health and safety procedures at all times
- Maintain machines, tools, and workshop equipment in good working order

---

### Requirements

- Proven experience as a Turner, Machinist, or Lathe Operator in a marine, industrial, or heavy engineering environment
- Practical experience in mechanical repair and maintenance activities
- Ability to read and interpret mechanical engineering drawings and technical documentation
- Proficiency in the use of precision measuring instruments
- Strong understanding of machining processes, dimensional tolerances, and surface finish requirements
- Knowledge of shipyard safety procedures and health and safety regulations
- Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and a methodical approach to problem-solving
- Ability to work independently and as part of a workshop team

---

### Preferred Qualifications

- Trade qualification or apprenticeship in machining, turning, or mechanical engineering
- Experience with CNC lathe programming and operation in addition to conventional turning
- Familiarity with marine classification society requirements for precision components
- Previous experience in a shipyard, dry dock, or marine repair workshop setting

### What's on Offer

- A professional and technically supportive workshop environment
