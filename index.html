Looking to hire?
Outreach Recruitment can help.

How does Outreach Recruitment help employers?

We partner with employers to find the right candidates quickly and efficiently. From understanding your hiring needs to screening, shortlisting, and supporting the onboarding process — we handle the recruitment so you can focus on running your business.

What types of roles does Outreach Recruitment fill?

We specialise in roles across Retail, Hospitality, Administration, People Operations, and more. Whether you need one person or an entire team, we match you with candidates who are ready to work and the right fit for your organisation.

How can I apply for a job through Outreach Recruitment?

Browse our current job openings at outreachrecruitment.eu/jobs and apply directly. Our team will review your application, contact you for a brief interview, and guide you through the process from start to finish.

What support do candidates receive?

We support candidates at every step — from preparing your CV and interview coaching to helping you understand your contract and settle into your new role. We stay in touch even after placement to make sure everything is going well.

What is the Study in Malta programme?

Our Study in Malta programme helps international students enrol in English language and vocational courses in Malta. We assist with school selection, enrolment, visa guidance, and accommodation so you can focus on your studies and experience.

Can I work while studying in Malta?

Yes, in many cases students can work part-time while studying in Malta. Our team will advise you on the specific rules that apply to your nationality and course, and we can connect you with suitable part-time opportunities through our recruitment network.

Where is Outreach Recruitment based?

Outreach Recruitment is based in Malta and operates across Malta and Europe. We connect local and international candidates with employers in Malta, Poland, Germany, and other European countries.

How do I get started with Outreach Recruitment?

Simply contact us through our website or book a call with our team. Whether you're an employer looking to hire or a candidate looking for work, we'll listen to your needs and find the best path forward together.
